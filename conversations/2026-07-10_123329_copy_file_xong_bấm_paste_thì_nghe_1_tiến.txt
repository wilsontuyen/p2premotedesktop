### Cause of the "Ding" Sound / Failed Paste
- In the previous session, a strict client-side verification check was added to `render_format` (measuring the age of `last_paste` relative to the current time, and matching it against the metadata arrival time).
- This check proved to be extremely fragile on the Client machine because:
  1. The user's interaction (e.g., right-clicking a folder, selecting "Paste") can easily span more than 3.0 seconds, violating the age check.
  2. If the keyboard or mouse hook polling missed a click due to UIPI focus boundaries, `last_paste_ready_time` remained uninitialized (`0.0`), failing the check.
- When `render_format` returned early on these failures, it returned `NULL` to the OS requesting clipboard data, triggering a Windows system "ding" sound and causing the paste operation to fail.
